# Xhibit AI — site plan

Source: the October 2026 site review plus the founders' decisions (17 Oct and 6 Nov 2026).
Progress is logged in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md).

## Decisions (fixed)

- **What we sell:** any software on the web — websites, web apps, mobile-optimised sites, landing pages, online stores, AI tools. No native Android/iOS apps.
- **Where we are:** don't market a "based in" city. Where a location must appear (footer, contact, privacy, search data) it is **Bengaluru**. Ranchi only appears in the vision line and in demo content.
- **No in-person visits**, no pricing, no founding offer, no founders/team section, no "concept project" labels.
- **No "live in 7 days" promise.** Talk about speed loosely ("live in days, not months"). Keep "WhatsApp reply within 2 hours".
- **Brand text (approved)**
  - Motto: *Made with you. Made to be seen.*
  - Vision: Every good idea deserves to be seen at its best, whether it's a café in Ranchi or a startup in Bengaluru.
  - Mission: We listen first, then design and build websites, web apps and AI tools around the way you work. Fast, honestly priced, and looked after long after launch.
  - Promises: See it before you pay · Built around your idea · Plain talk and written quotes · Yours to keep: domain, code and data in your name
- **Contact:** WhatsApp +91 92639 30093 · Phone +91 88093 87090 · contact.xhibitai@gmail.com · domain not bought yet.
- **Type:** Redaction (display) replaces Fraunces completely; Plus Jakarta Sans stays for body.
- **Colour:** neutral porcelain-white background, black text, several accent hues instead of one orange.
